02 / Prompts

Prompts that pull their weight.

FULL LIBRARY → PROMPTS PAGE, SOON

Run this before any campaign build. It attacks a fuzzy ICP definition from the buyer's side until only the segments worth money survive.

You are a skeptical CRO reviewing my ICP definition before approving GTM budget.

My product: {{one-sentence product description}}
My claimed ICP: {{current ICP definition}}
Evidence I have: {{closed-won patterns, interviews, usage data}}

Do four passes:
1. List every assumption hiding in my ICP that the evidence does not support.
2. For each assumption, state what data would confirm or kill it, and the cheapest way to get that data this week.
3. Split my ICP into sub-segments by urgency of pain, not firmographics. Rank them by expected close rate × deal size.
4. Rewrite the ICP as one paragraph a new SDR could act on without asking questions.

Be blunt. If a segment only exists because I want it to, say so.

One input, twelve testable emails. Angles × lengths as a proper test matrix, so replies tell you *why* something worked, not just that it did.

Build me a cold email test matrix.

Offer: {{what I'm selling and the concrete outcome it buys}}
Recipient: {{role + company type}}
Proof: {{best 1–2 proof points, with numbers}}

Generate 12 emails: 4 angles × 3 lengths.
Angles: (A) pain agitation, (B) peer proof, (C) contrarian insight about their market, (D) blunt direct ask.
Lengths: 50, 90, and 140 words.

Rules: subject lines under 5 words, lowercase. No "hope you're well," no "I know you're busy," no exclamation points. One CTA per email — a question, not a calendar link. Write like a colleague, not a vendor.

Output as a table: ID, angle, length, subject, body. Then predict which two win and say why.

A conversion audit that grades the page the way a distracted buyer experiences it — five seconds at a time — and hands back a prioritized fix list.

Audit this landing page copy as a conversion strategist.

Page copy: {{paste full page copy, top to bottom}}
Traffic source: {{where visitors come from + their intent}}
Conversion goal: {{the one action that counts}}

1. Five-second test: based on the hero alone, what would a visitor say this company does, for whom, and why it's different? Flag every gap between that and the truth.
2. Score 1–5 with evidence: clarity, relevance to the traffic source, proof/trust, friction, CTA strength.
3. Walk the page as the skeptical buyer: at each section, write the objection they're silently raising and whether the next section answers it.
4. Output a fix list ranked by (impact × ease), with rewritten copy for the top 3 fixes.
