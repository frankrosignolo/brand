Typography

The serif (Source Serif 4, the fin.ai register) leads headlines in sentence case. Display XL is medium (500); everything else is light or regular — the serif never goes bold. Archivo is the body voice: regular, italic, medium, and a sparing body-only bold. Space Mono carries metadata. Caps never touch the serif.

Illustration

Copperplate line engraving — the banknote-portrait (intaglio) tradition. Tone is drawn, never printed: engraved lines that swell, taper and curve with the form, cross-hatched where shadow deepens. It is a pure-line style — no halftone dots, no gray fills, and not a stipple "hedcut."

05
Engraving tone scale DENSITY = VALUE
Highlight
Light
Mid
Shadow
Core

Tone is built by adding hatch directions and closing the spacing — highlight is bare paper with a few sparse strokes; the core shadow is three or four crossed sets. Never a flat gray fill, never a halftone dot screen.

Rules

Ink, not black. Strokes are ink-900 on paper — warm, never pure #000/#fff.

One spot of rust. Optional single-element accent or a full rust duotone. Never full color.

Lines follow form. Strokes ride the surface like a contour map — around a cheek, along a fold. Cross-hatch only where shadow needs it; stipple is a filler accent, never the base.

For images — every portrait or photo asset gets this treatment: convert to engraving-style linework (commissioned engraver, scratchboard scan, or a photo→line-engraving conversion), place on a white or transparent ground, normalize strokes to ink-900, cap at one rust move.
